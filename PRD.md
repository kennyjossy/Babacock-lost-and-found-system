# BABCOCK UNIVERSITY LOST AND FOUND MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

## 1. Introduction 

A Lost and Found Management System is a system that allows students to report their missing items and also allocate staff to assist the student in finding the missing items. 
Students might not know where or to whom to report their missing items.

## 2. Problem Statement

Students may become worried, nervous and may not know where to report the missing item, so the students need assistance from the appropriate university staff. Students start to search around the campus.
 Asking people around is not always enough, student may waste a lot time asking differen tpeople without getting useful information. However, the appropriate staff can collect important information such as the last place the student saw the item, a picture of the item and student's details.
Having an online reporting system can help students report their missing items, state where they last saw the item, describe what it looks like and receives assistance from the appropriate staff.

## 3. Target Users
Students: Students who lose or find personal belongings on campus.
University Staff: Staff members who help students recover missing items.
Security Personel: Security officers who assist in receiving and identifying recovered items.

## 4. Proposed Solution
The proposed Lost and Found Management System will provide an online platform where students, university staff, and security personnel can report lost and found belongings.
Users should be able to:
1. Mention the name or type of item they have lost or found.
2. Describe what the item looks like.
3. State the last location where they saw the item.
4. Provide contact details, such as their phone number and matric number.
5. Users should be able to upload item pictures.
6. Contact the appropriate university staff for assistance.

## 5. Key Features of the System 
1. User login.
2. Lost item report.
3. Found item report
4. Picture Upload
5. Contact Information
6. Item Search
7. Report Tracking
8. Staff Assistance


## 6. User Requirements
The System should allow the users to:
1. Create an account and log in.
2. Report missing items.
3. Report items they have found.
4. Upload pictures of lost or found items.
5. Describe the item.
6. Enter the last location it was seen.
7. Provide their contact information.
8. Search for reported items.
9. Receive assistantance from university staff
10. Check the status of their reports.

## 7. System Requirements
The System will require following:
1. A computer or Smartphone to access the platforms.
2. An internet connection.
3. A web browser to open the system.
4. A database to store lost and found item records.
5. A secure login system to protect users' information.
6. An online platforms for communication between users and university staff.

## 8. Expected Benefits
The Lost and Found Management System will provide the following benefits:
1. It will make reporting missing items easier.
2. It will reduce the time spentearching for missing items
3. It will help users locate their losts items.
4. It makes it easier to identify and contact people who found missing items.
5. It improves communication between users and university staff.
6. It helps university staff manage lost and found reports.

## 9. Conclusion
The Babcock University Lost and Found Management System is proposed to make it easier for students and staff to report and recover lost items.

## 10. Future Improvements
A mobile application for easier access.
